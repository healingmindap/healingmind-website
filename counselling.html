What we help with

Whatever you are carrying, you do not have to work through it alone.

Anxiety & worry

Constant worry, panic attacks, overthinking, nervousness and fears that get in the way of daily life.

Low mood & depression

Persistent sadness, emptiness, loss of interest, low energy or hopelessness.

Stress & burnout

Pressure from work, studies, family or life changes that is affecting your health and sleep.

Sleep difficulties

Trouble falling asleep, staying asleep, disturbed sleep or sleeping too much.

Relationship & marriage

Conflict, distance or communication problems — individually, as a couple, before or after marriage.

Family concerns

Family therapy to improve understanding and resolve long-running conflicts together.

Anger management

Understand your triggers and build practical skills to stay calm and communicate better.

Difficult experiences

A gentle, paced space to process painful past events and rebuild a sense of safety.
